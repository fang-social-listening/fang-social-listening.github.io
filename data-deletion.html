Data Deletion Instructions

How to delete your data from Fang

If you are a Page admin who connected a Page:

  1. In Fang, open Settings → Facebook Pages → click Disconnect. Fang stops receiving data from that Page straight away.
  2. Or on Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove Fang.
  3. To delete everything already stored, email fang.team.nano@gmail.com with subject "Delete my data" and your Page name. We delete it within 30 days and reply with a confirmation code.

If you commented on a Page that uses Fang:

Email fang.team.nano@gmail.com with the Page name and a link to (or screenshot of) your comment. We delete it from Fang within 30 days and confirm by email.

Questions: fang.team.nano@gmail.com. Fang Social Listening (company registration in Thailand in progress), Bangkok, Thailand.
