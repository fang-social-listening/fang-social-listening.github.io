Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Fang Labs (company registration in Thailand in progress) ("Fang Labs", "we") provides Fang Labs, a customer-care tool that helps businesses answer the comments and Messenger messages their customers send to the business's own Facebook Pages. This policy explains what data we process when a business connects its Page to Fang Labs.

Who is responsible

The business that connects its Page (our customer) is the data controller for the comments and messages people send to its Page. Fang Labs processes that data only on the customer's instructions, under a data processing agreement, as a data processor under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

Data we receive from Facebook

When a Page admin connects a Page through Facebook Login, with their permission we receive:

  • the list of Pages they manage, and an access token for the Page(s) they select (stored encrypted);
  • posts published by the connected Page (text, image link, time, whether it is an ad);
  • comments people leave on the connected Page's posts: comment text, time, comment ID, and the commenter's public name/ID where Facebook provides it;
  • Messenger conversations between people and the connected Page: message text, attachments (image/file links), time, the person's Page-scoped ID and the name/profile picture Facebook returns, and the ad a conversation started from, if any;
  • replies and messages the Page sends through Fang Labs.

We do not access personal profiles, friends lists, Facebook Groups, or any Page that was not connected.

Data the business's staff create in Fang Labs

Staff names, work emails and roles; internal notes, tags, assignments, saved replies, order details a customer typed (such as a phone number or delivery address) and an audit log of actions (who replied, hid, deleted or changed what, and when).

How we use it

  • to show comments and messages to the customer's staff in a shared inbox, with queues, assignment and response-time targets;
  • to let staff reply as the Page, and, when the customer turns it on, to hide or delete spam and scam comments on the customer's own Page (hiding is reversible; deleting needs a confirmation; every action is logged);
  • to alert the customer's own team (for example in their LINE group) about urgent complaints or late replies, when the customer connects LINE;
  • when the customer turns on AI features, to label comments and messages (topic, sentiment, urgency) and to draft replies that staff review before sending;
  • to produce reports for the customer about volume, topics and response times.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or combine one customer's data with another's. A customer's data improves only that customer's own workspace.

AI processing

AI features are off by default and are switched on per workspace by the customer's admin. When on, the text of comments and messages (and the related post caption) is sent to OpenAI to produce labels and draft replies. Under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train OpenAI's models. Staff always review a draft before anything is sent; the AI never posts on its own.

Service providers (sub-processors)

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Messenger): the source of the data and the channel for replies.
  • OpenAI (USA): AI labels and drafts, only when the customer turns AI on.
  • LINE Corporation: alerts to the customer's own LINE group, only when the customer connects LINE.
  • Hosting and network: Cloudflare (secure tunnel) during the pilot; Render (Singapore) for production.
  • An email provider, to send sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails.

Transfers outside Thailand rely on the safeguards in PDPA sections 28–29 and the providers' own terms.

Retention

Comments, messages and their notes are kept for the period the customer sets (default 365 days), then deleted automatically. When a customer disconnects a Page, or Meta sends us a deletion request, that Page's comments, messages and case history are deleted at once. The text of a comment deleted from Facebook through Fang Labs stays only in the audit log for 30 days, then is erased. Encrypted backups are kept for 14 days. Access logs required by law are kept for up to 90 days.

Deleting your data

  • Page admins: click "Disconnect" in Fang Labs, or remove Fang Labs under Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. Then email fang.team.nano@gmail.com to ask for full deletion.
  • People who commented on or messaged a Page: email fang.team.nano@gmail.com with the Page name and a link to, or screenshot of, your comment or conversation. We delete it from Fang Labs within 30 days and confirm in writing. Deleting a comment on Facebook also removes it from new Fang Labs views.

Full instructions: data-deletion.html

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Page tokens and backups are encrypted at rest. Each customer workspace is kept separate, passwords are hashed, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and access and changes are logged. Details: our security overview, available on request.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection. For data a business holds about you, contact that business; we help them respond. You can also contact fang.team.nano@gmail.com, or complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).
