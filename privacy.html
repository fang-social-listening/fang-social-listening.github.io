Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Fang Social Listening (company registration in Thailand in progress) ("Fang", "we") provides Fang, a customer-care tool that helps businesses monitor and respond to comments on their own Facebook Pages. This policy explains what data we process when a business connects its Facebook Page to Fang.

Who is responsible

The business that connects its Page (our customer) is the data controller for comments left on its Page. Fang processes that data on the customer's behalf, under a data processing agreement, as a data processor under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

Data we receive from Facebook

When a Page admin connects a Page through Facebook Login, with their permission we receive:

  • the list of Pages they manage, and an access token for the Page(s) they select;
  • posts published by the connected Page (text, link, time);
  • comments that people leave on the connected Page's posts: comment text, time, comment ID, and the commenter's public name/ID where Facebook provides it;
  • replies the Page publishes through Fang.

We do not access personal profiles, private messages (unless a future Messenger feature is separately enabled by the customer), Groups, or friends lists.

How we use it

  • to show comments to the customer's staff in a case queue and classify them (e.g. question, complaint, spam link);
  • to alert the customer's moderators (e.g. via LINE) about urgent complaints;
  • to let the customer's staff reply to a comment as the Page, and to post replies the customer approved in advance (for example outside office hours);
  • to produce reports for the customer about response times and common topics.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or combine one customer's data with another's. A customer's data improves only that customer's own account.

Service providers

To run the service we use: our cloud hosting provider; OpenAI (USA) to classify comment text (API data is not used to train their models); LINE (alerts to the customer's moderators); Meta (the Facebook platform). Transfers outside Thailand rely on the safeguards in PDPA sections 28–29.

Retention

We keep comment data for as long as the customer's contract runs, then delete it within 30 days of termination or of a deletion request, except access logs we must keep for 90 days under the Computer Crime Act.

Deleting your data

  • Page admins: click "Disconnect" in Fang, or remove Fang under Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. Then email fang.team.nano@gmail.com to ask for full deletion.
  • People who commented on a Page: email fang.team.nano@gmail.com with the Page name and a link to your comment. We delete it from Fang within 30 days and confirm in writing. Deleting a comment on Facebook also removes it from new Fang views.

Full instructions: data-deletion.html

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each customer is kept separate, access is limited to authorised staff, and access is logged.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Contact fang.team.nano@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).
